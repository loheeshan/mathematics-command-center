# Foundation Gap Report

Prerequisites that the university notes **assume** but do not teach (or teach in one line). Each gap is linked to the Master Core topic that bridges it. Bridge availability (lesson / lab / practice) is computed live in `coverage-report.md` — this report lists the *need*, not a claim that the bridge exists.

The audience includes learners who studied biology (not mathematics) at advanced level, so "assumed" includes school algebra.
